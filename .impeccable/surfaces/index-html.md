# Surface: Home (index.html)

Scope: studio home / hub that routes visitors to /influencers, /medicos, /mentores. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience & job: influencers, médicos, mentores arriving (often mobile, from social) need to recognise "this studio is for me" and enter their landing or start a project.
Action: "Ver planes" → profile selector → landing; secondary "Iniciar proyecto" (email).
Proof on hand: none real yet; stats, works and testimonials are labelled placeholders.
Constraints: brief-pinned world (no concept roll; direction pinned by user). Code-led (no image generation).

## Direction contract

THESIS: Attention is the product, so the page literally looks back. A living RAKÜN eye opens the site, settles into the punk raccoon and keeps watching from header, hero, manifesto and CTA. Refuses the agency default of a centred headline over a row of service cards.

OWN-WORLD: Navy #1B1A35 night fields alternating with cream #F3F0EA editorial sheets; pink #FF2C68 always protagonist, yellow/blue as accents; 1px hairline grid, margin rail with 001–007 indices, "+" registration crosses, mono coordinates and Bogotá clock; diagonal cuts, pink spike bursts, iris rings (blue-yellow-pink); Knuckle Down caps headlines, Futura-light tracked labels.

STORY: Visitor meets the eye (intro), reads "DISEÑO QUE TE HACE MIRAR.", recognises their profile in three color-coded cards, absorbs the manifesto, sees process/work/proof, and picks their profile under "¿LISTO PARA QUE TE VEAN?".

FIRST VIEWPORT: Navy. Header strip (badge, live mini-eye, nav, clock, pink CTA). Left 7 cols: three-line headline ~10vw, last line pink, sub-copy and underlined link. Right: collage — raccoon isotype with live cursor-following eye over blue/yellow blocks, B/W diagonal-cut portraits, pink crest spikes, mono figure labels. Rail "001" on the left edge.

FORM: Pinned brief (editorial-brutalist studio site in RAKÜN palette); position n/a; seed key: none (direction pinned by brief, concept-seed skipped by rule).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
